Brand Voice

Wolfburn's voice rules. Voice (the agent) checks every output before it leaves the system.

Voice rules · approved

  • DO Speak with quiet confidence. Wolfburn doesn't shout.
  • DO Lead with the place: Thurso, Caithness, the Pentland Firth.
  • DO Use "single malt Scotch whisky" not "scotch" or "whiskey" (American spelling).
  • DON'T No price-led promotion language: "discount", "sale", "limited time" feel cheap.
  • DON'T Avoid clichés: "smooth", "approachable", "easy drinker", "perfect for".
  • DON'T Never use em-dashes in copy (Wesley's preference). Use commas, colons, periods.
