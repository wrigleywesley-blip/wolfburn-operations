WELCOME TO WOLFBURN

SINGLE MALT SCOTCH WHISKY FROM THE FAR NORTH OF SCOTLAND

EXPLORE OUR WHISKIES

Situated on the rugged north coast of Scotland, Wolfburn Distillery was originally founded in 1821 and quickly became the largest distillery in Caithness. Today, we honour that heritage by crafting our award-winning single malt Scotch whisky the traditional way – by hand, using the finest natural ingredients, and never rushing the process.

Every drop is distilled, matured, and bottled on-site at our Highland distillery in Thurso, where the wild elements of the north coast shape the character of our spirit. The results are whiskies that are smooth, complex, and deeply connected to our origins. Learn more about us here.
