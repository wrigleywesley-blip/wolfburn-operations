Four production-ready Meta campaign blueprints. Three match-specific campaigns activate around each Scotland fixture (Haiti, Morocco, Brazil) with the matching bottle as the hero product. A Appendix · tracking, budget, benchmarks

Tracking + budget + benchmarks.

Pixel events, UTM convention, budget allocation across the four campaigns, performance benchmarks to call winners by day 7.

Pixel events to track

Standard events
  • PageView · all PDPs · for retargeting + LAL seed
  • ViewContent · individual product PDPs (10yr, 8yr, 12yr, box)
  • AddToCart · cart_id, value, currency
  • InitiateCheckout · funnel midpoint
  • Purchase · value, currency, content_ids, content_type=product
Custom conversions
  • WC26_BoxPurchase · Purchase event where content_ids contains the box SKU
  • WC26_BottlePurchase · Purchase where content_ids matches any of the three individual bottles
  • WC26_NewsletterSignup · footer / PDP email capture form submit

UTM parameter convention

URL pattern

?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=wc26_match[1-3] | wc26_umbrella&utm_content=ad[1-4]_[static|carousel|reel|video]&utm_term=headline_var[1-3]

Example: ?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=wc26_match1&utm_content=ad1_static&utm_term=headline_var2

Total budget allocation

Spend split across the 4 campaigns£7,000 total
  • Campaign 1 · Haiti · 10YR · £1,200 (17%)
  • Campaign 2 · Morocco · 8YR · £900 (13%)
  • Campaign 3 · Brazil · 12YR · £1,400 (20%)
  • Campaign 4 · Umbrella · The Box · £3,500 (50%)

Total: £7,000 across 27 days · average £259/day blended · the umbrella campaign carries the largest share because it runs the longest and sells the highest-AOV product.

Performance benchmarks · call winners by day 7

Targets per campaign
  • ROAS · target 3.0× blended across all four campaigns
  • CTR · feed static · > 1.8% (Wolfburn historical baseline 1.6%)
  • CTR · reels · > 1.2%
  • CPM · < £14 UK · < £18 US
  • CPC · < £0.80 UK
  • Conversion rate (PDP → purchase) · > 2.4% (storefront baseline 1.99%)

If any ad creative is below target after 48 hours and £100 spend, pause it. If a campaign is below target after 72 hours, refresh creative (don't kill the campaign yet). Refresh creative every 7 days regardless of performance to avoid fatigue.
