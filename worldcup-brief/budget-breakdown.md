## Why these ratios within the £25 cap

1. **60/40 split during Haiti and Morocco windows**, gives the umbrella the majority share (because the collection is the highest-AOV product) while still giving each match-day campaign enough budget to land. £10/day × 9 days for Haiti = £90 in total, which is meaningful match-day reach.
2. **48/52 split during the Brazil window** — only phase where the match-day campaign slightly outspends the umbrella. Brazil is the marquee fixture, hardest creative to land, broadest cultural curiosity. The skew is deliberate.
3. **100% umbrella during all quiet phases**, when no match campaign is active, the full £25 goes to the collection. The collection never drops below £12/day at any point. Umbrella is the workhorse.
4. **Stop dead after each match** — no promoting a match-specific bottle after that match has been played. The hook is gone. Spend rolls back to the umbrella.
5. **Match-day viewers retarget into the umbrella pool** — every person who sees a match-specific ad becomes warm audience for the umbrella. Spike-then-sweep.
