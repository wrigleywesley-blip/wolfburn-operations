4 ANGLES · 12 AD CREATIVES · 1 PRODUCT

Four ways to sell one wooden box.

Four distinct creative angles for the Drams for the Tartan Army wooden box collection. Built to test against each other and find the highest-ROAS angle, then scale the winner. For Wolfburn team review and sign-off before we set up in Meta Ads Manager.

What's in this document

Four ad-set angles for the wooden box collection. Angle A · The Moment leads on the World Cup. Angle B · The Craft leads on heritage and place. Angle C · The Scarcity leads on the limited release. Angle D · The Gift leads on the gifting and Father's Day moment. Each angle has 3 creatives (static hero, carousel, reel). All four run against each other on a small test budget for 5-7 days, the winner takes the bulk of the spend.

⚡ Strategy pivot · 27 May

One product, four angles.

Following review with the Wolfburn team, we are dropping the individual-bottle campaigns and focusing all paid spend on the Drams for the Tartan Army wooden box. The data has been clear: customers are buying the box, not the individual bottles. The campaign architecture simplifies to one product, multiple creative angles, one budget pool.

This document is for the Wolfburn team to review and approve the copy before we set up the campaigns in Meta. The approval checklist sits at the bottom of the document.

Budget · test & scale

All four angles get equal share for 5 days. After day 5 we kill the bottom 2 and scale the top 2 for the rest of the tournament window.
Phase 1 · the test (days 1-5)
  • £25/day total · split equally across 4 ad sets via CBO (algo distributes based on early performance)
  • ~£6.25/day per angle as the soft floor
  • 5-day test gives us ~£125 total spend · enough volume to call winners at our typical conversion rate
Phase 2 · scale (day 6 onwards)
  • Pause the 2 lowest-ROAS ad sets
  • Reallocate the full £25/day to the top 2 winners · ~£12.50/day each
  • Refresh creative every 7 days within those top 2 to avoid fatigue
  • Run continuously through tournament window and beyond
Phase 3 · post-tournament (26 Jun onwards)
  • Continue running winning angle(s) at £25/day until box sells through
  • Switch CTA from "Reserve" to "Shop Now" once on storefront
  • Add seasonal retargeting overlays (e.g., "Last chance · Father's Day")

Picking the winner · what we measure

Decision criteria after 5 days of test. Ranked priority.
Metrics in priority order
  • 1 · ROAS · primary indicator. Target 3.0× minimum.
  • 2 · Cost per box purchase · target < £18 UK · < £24 international
  • 3 · CTR feed static · target > 1.8% (Wolfburn historical baseline 1.6%)
  • 4 · CTR reels · target > 1.2%
  • 5 · Add-to-cart rate · target > 4.5% of clicks
  • 6 · Comments + shares · qualitative read on whether the copy is landing
